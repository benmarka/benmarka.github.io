About the Author

Writing at the edge of myth and history

Aaron Benmark is a fiction writer working across historical fantasy and science fiction. His work is drawn to the long view — civilizations in motion, the weight of centuries, characters who survive long enough to see everything change.

He is currently deep in the second volume of Children of the Wolf, his twelve-book Roman epic narrated by the immortal Remus.
